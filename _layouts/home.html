Statistics Fundamentals Explained

Statistics is the science of collecting, analysing, interpreting, and presenting data. It plays a crucial role in modern decision-making across industries like business, science, and technology.

The field is divided into descriptive statistics, which summarizes data, and inferential statistics, which helps make predictions based on data samples.

Descriptive Statistics

Summarize data using mean, median, and standard deviation.

Inferential Statistics

Draw conclusions about populations using sample data.

Probability

Understand uncertainty and likelihood of events.

Data Visualization

Present insights using charts and graphs.

Why Statistics Matters

Statistics helps individuals and organizations make informed decisions, identify trends, and solve real-world problems using data.
